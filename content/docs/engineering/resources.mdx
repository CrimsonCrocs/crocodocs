Choose a reference for the design question you need to answer. Use official rules for legality, manufacturer documentation for component ratings, and handbooks for explanations and examples.

## Handbooks [#start-with-these-two-handbooks]

| Resource | Best use | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Game Manual 0](https://gm0.org/en/latest/) | Broad FTC design and mechanism explanations, diagrams, and examples | Community guidance; historical examples and advice need context |
| [GM0 useful resources](https://gm0.org/en/latest/docs/useful-resources.html) | Finding deeper references, CAD libraries, calculators, and team resources | Some outgoing links move or become outdated |
| [FTC Wiki](https://www.ftcwiki.org/) | Assembly, material selection, construction styles, and manufacturing concepts | Some pages are incomplete; verify equations and specifications independently |

## Rules and specifications [#use-primary-sources-for-changing-requirements]

- [FIRST game and season materials](https://www.firstinspires.org/resource-library/ftc/game-and-season-info): current manuals, updates, and inspection information.
- [Official FTC Q&A](https://ftc-qa.firstinspires.org/): published rule clarifications for the relevant season.
- [FTC Docs](https://ftc-docs.firstinspires.org/en/latest/): official configuration, programming, build, and troubleshooting guidance.
- [FIRST Robot Wiring Guide](https://ftc-docs.firstinspires.org/en/latest/robot_building/wiring_guide/wiring-guide.html): electrical layout, connections, and integration.
- [FIRST ESD guidance](https://ftc-docs.firstinspires.org/en/latest/hardware_and_software_configuration/configuring/managing_esd/managing-esd.html): supported discharge mitigation and the distinction between team and event-host actions.

Check part numbers against the current rules before building around a parts list or a robot you saw in a video.

## Hardware references [#look-up-the-hardware-you-actually-own]

- [REV documentation](https://docs.revrobotics.com/): controller specifications, build instructions, actuator details, and troubleshooting. Use the page for the exact product.
- [REV battery guidance](https://docs.revrobotics.com/duo-control/troubleshooting-the-control-system/12v-battery-best-practices-and-troubleshooting): handling and troubleshooting context for REV's 12 V system.
- [goBILDA](https://www.gobilda.com/): product drawings, dimensions, ratios, compatible hardware, and build instructions attached to specific products.
- [KHK gear reference](https://khkgears.net/gear-knowledge/gear-technical-reference/calculation-gear-dimensions/): gear geometry, reference dimensions, and distinctions between gear systems.
- [SDP/SI timing-belt handbook](https://sdp-si.com/D820/PDFS/Technical-Section.pdf): pulley geometry, center distance, tension, alignment, and drive selection. Use [GM0's calculator](https://gm0.org/en/latest/docs/common-mechanisms/power-transmission/belt.html#belt-calculator) for an initial belt-length comparison.

Use datasheets for voltage, current, torque, temperature, and load ratings. An illustrative calculation in this handbook does not override a component's rating.

## CAD and manufacturing [#learn-cad-and-design-for-manufacture]

- [Onshape Learning Center](https://learn.onshape.com/): tool-specific instruction in modeling and assemblies.
- [FTC Onshape library](https://ftconshape.com/): discover reusable models and CAD workflow resources. Check critical geometry against the supplier drawing.
- [Onshape for FIRST Robotics teams](https://www.onshape.com/en/education/robotics): education access, team onboarding, robotics training, and FTC model links. Verify the model's season and the account's plan eligibility.
- [Prusa design-for-printing guide](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/modeling-with-3d-printing-in-mind_164135): orientation, feature design, and iteration.
- [Prusa filament material guide](https://help.prusa3d.com/filament-material-guide): material and printer requirements; qualify the actual part separately.
- [NASA RAP Robotics Design Guide](https://robotics.nasa.gov/nasa-rap-robotics-design-guide/): a broader competitive-robotics reference linked by GM0. It includes other competition contexts, so separate general engineering from FTC-specific assumptions.

For a first exercise, use the [sensor-bracket CAD task](/docs/engineering/design/cad). For a fit problem, use the [tolerance coupon method](/docs/engineering/manufacturing/fits-and-tolerances). For a structural print, use the [print testing guide](/docs/engineering/manufacturing/3d-printing).

## Engineering theory

- [Thaddeus Hughes: Mechatronics](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Thaddeus-Maximus/mechatronics_book/master/mechatronics.pdf): a broad reference for mechanisms, electronics, and measurement. It contains some technical errors; check specifications and equations against the exact manufacturer reference before applying them.
- [Bartosz Ciechanowski: Gears](https://ciechanow.ski/gears/): interactive explanations of pitch circles, tooth profiles, pressure angle, and gear trains. Use it to understand the geometry, then use the supplier's ratings for a real assembly.

## Testing and community support [#keep-evidence-and-ask-useful-questions]

- [Community portfolio library](https://portfolios.hivemindrobotics.net/): examples of engineering communication. Treat them as authored work and check the season before applying submission conventions.

When asking a mentor, vendor, or community for help, provide the exact hardware, a labeled photo or sketch, the observed symptom, the expected behavior, and the tests already performed. A short video showing the whole motion is often more useful than a close-up of the failed endpoint.

When references disagree, use the current official rules for legality and the manufacturer’s documentation for hardware ratings.
