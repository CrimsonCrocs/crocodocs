A portfolio tells judges what your team built, learned, and achieved during the season. Use it to support **every award your team pursues**, with photos, results, and explanations of your decisions.

## First portfolio

Start with a robot improvement, something a member learned and applied, and an outreach project with a recorded result. Gather the photos, measurements, dates, and notes before laying out pages.

Then work through this sequence:

1. [Awards and Inspire](/docs/portfolio/awards-and-inspire): identify the evidence your team needs.
2. [Planning](/docs/portfolio/planning): choose the stories and assign pages.
3. [Engineering](/docs/portfolio/engineering), [programming](/docs/portfolio/programming), [outreach](/docs/portfolio/outreach), and [team development](/docs/portfolio/team): turn records into explanations.
4. [Layout and writing](/docs/portfolio/layout): make those explanations easy to read.
5. [Interviews](/docs/portfolio/interviews) and [final review](/docs/portfolio/review): check that the document and the team are ready.

You can start with a kit robot, a small local partnership, and a simple document. Describe the work your team actually did and the choices you understand.

## Improving a portfolio

| If your draft has… | Start with… |
| --- | --- |
| A finished robot with little explanation of how it developed | [Engineering evidence](/docs/portfolio/engineering) |
| A list of libraries and sensors | [Programming evidence](/docs/portfolio/programming) |
| Event photos and large audience totals | [Outreach and impact](/docs/portfolio/outreach) |
| A roster and sponsor logos, but little evidence of team growth | [Team development](/docs/portfolio/team) |
| Crowded pages or long paragraphs | [Layout and writing](/docs/portfolio/layout) |
| Strong individual sections but unclear Inspire coverage | [Awards and Inspire](/docs/portfolio/awards-and-inspire) |
