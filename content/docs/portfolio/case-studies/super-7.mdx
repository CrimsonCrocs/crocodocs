Super 7, team 7477, won the **Edison Division Inspire Award at the 2026 FIRST Championship**. Its DECODE portfolio uses timelines, project examples, and side-by-side designs to explain the season.

## Outreach projects

Page 5 gives an outreach overview, while page 6 spends more space on individual projects. The reader gets both the scale of the work and a closer look at what the team did.

Each project has a name, a short explanation, and a picture or other detail to help the reader place it. Recurring partnerships and published resources can be understood as separate pieces of work.

A timeline can cover your team's smaller activities. Use the space it saves to explain a few projects in more detail: why you chose them, what students did, and what happened afterward.

The repeated layout helps the engineering explanation. Instead of searching several pages, readers can move across one row and see how the mechanism changed. The next page develops the indexer investigation in more detail.

Try this for a mechanism that went through several versions. Use the same view and comparison points for each, then explain what prompted the next change.

## Team planning

Page 4 pairs problems the team might face with its plans for handling them. These include graduating members, missed deadlines, new recruits, and unavailable mentors. The recruitment and training diagrams show how the team prepares newer members to take responsibility.

A table like this can make a sustainability plan easier to understand. Start with a problem your team actually faces, explain what you are doing about it, and show how you check progress.

## Control testing

Page 15 explains a launcher problem: the flywheel slows after a shot, affecting the next one. Before-and-after plots sit beside the controller explanation, with the team reporting recovery time improving from roughly 1.2 seconds to 0.15 seconds.

The page connects the controller to a visible change in the robot's behavior. For your own control feature, put the problem, code or control idea, and test result together. That gives readers a reason to care about the algorithm you used.
