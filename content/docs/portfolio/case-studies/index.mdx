## Reading the studies

Page references mean **numbered content pages**, excluding the cover. In these copies, content page 1 is PDF page 2. This distinction helps when moving between an article and a PDF viewer.

Award placements were checked against FIRST's event records. Robot performance, outreach totals, and project outcomes are **reported by the teams**. Their tests and activity records have not been independently audited for these reviews.

The award records confirm the results. They do not tell us which pages influenced the judges.

## Applying the examples

Start with a problem in your draft. Find a similar page in an example and compare how the team explains its design change, training, or outreach result. Use that approach to organize your own work.

Follow the current BIOBUZZ criteria when preparing your submission. Write your own text and use your own photographs, diagrams, and identity. The original PDFs and page artwork are not redistributed here.
