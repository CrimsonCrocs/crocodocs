Four Inspire portfolios show different ways to explain a season. Each study includes page images and points out useful choices in writing, layout, and organisation.

## The portfolios

| Team | Portfolio season | Verified Championship result | Useful focus |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Rebel Robotics, 18139](/docs/portfolio/case-studies/rebel-robotics) | INTO THE DEEP, 2024–25 | Overall Inspire winner, 2025 | Award overview, engineering changes, and regional partnerships |
| [Blu Cru, 6417](/docs/portfolio/case-studies/blu-cru) | DECODE, 2025–26 | Goodall Division Inspire winner, 2026 | Applied mentor learning, design analysis, and software recovery |
| [Super 7, 7477](/docs/portfolio/case-studies/super-7) | DECODE, 2025–26 | Edison Division Inspire winner, 2026 | Team risks, recurring outreach, and mechanism comparisons |
| [Seattle Solvers, 23511](/docs/portfolio/case-studies/seattle-solvers) | DECODE, 2025–26 | Lovelace Division Inspire second place, 2026 | Training progression, measurable design goals, and iteration |

## Reading the studies

Page numbers refer to the portfolio's numbered content pages, excluding the cover. Select a page image to open it at full size. The full portfolios are linked in each article's Sources section.

## Applying the examples

Start with something you want to explain better: a design change, member training, or an outreach project. Look at how another team organised a similar topic, then use the idea to tell your own team's story.

The examples come from earlier seasons, so use current award and submission requirements when preparing your portfolio.
