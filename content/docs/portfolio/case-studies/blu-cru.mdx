Blu Cru, team 6417, won the **Goodall Division Inspire Award at the 2026 FIRST Championship**. Its DECODE portfolio connects member learning to robot design and uses diagrams to explain mechanisms and driver controls.

## Learning put to use

Pages 3 and 8 describe skills the team wanted to develop, the people who helped, and the work members did afterward. Manufacturing, vision, and design analysis appear again in the engineering pages, so the training has a visible result.

Page 10 brings that work together in a design process: requirements, ideas, prototypes, CAD, simulation, and testing.

Examples such as corner relief for milling and a fixture for a thin plate show how the team applied manufacturing knowledge to real parts. These details give the learning story something concrete to point to.

In your portfolio, follow one skill through to a finished task. Explain what a mentor taught, then show what a student made or changed with that knowledge.

## Mechanism development

Pages 11–13 place drawings and calculations beside the mechanisms they explain. The transfer page follows several linkage versions; the launcher page connects a calculation to the operating range the team was considering.

The explanation stays close to the design question. A reader can see the mechanism, the problem being worked on, and how the analysis helped. Use the same arrangement when a calculation helped your team choose a ratio, size a part, or change a mechanism.

## Driver controls and recovery

Page 15 uses a state diagram to show the normal driver cycle, with routes to manual control and a failsafe. The aiming explanation beside it shows how an initial estimate from robot position is refined with camera feedback.

The diagram makes the sequence easier to follow than a long block of code. Arrows show what starts an action, what happens next, and how a driver can recover when needed.

For your robot, draw the normal sequence first. Add the sensor checks and manual controls that change it, then explain how you tested those paths.
