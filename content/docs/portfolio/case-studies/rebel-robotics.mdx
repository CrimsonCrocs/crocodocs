Rebel Robotics, team 18139, won the **overall Inspire Award at the 2025 FIRST Championship**, along with the Franklin Division award. Its INTO THE DEEP portfolio makes a large amount of work easy to find, then explains how the robot developed through the season.

## Award overview

The first content page introduces the team and robot, groups highlights by award, and gives page references for the longer explanations. Judges can see the main achievements before choosing where to read more.

The overview works because the highlights lead somewhere. A short statement about an achievement points to a page with the design, test, or project behind it. You can use that approach to introduce your own team without trying to explain the whole season on the first page.

<Callout type="warn" title="Check local judging guidance">

“Judged Category Highlights” pages are common in U.S. portfolios, but their headings and page references can resemble a table of contents. This format may be discouraged in other regions, so check local judging guidance before using it.

</Callout>

## Robot changes

Pages 9–10 follow the robot from its early competitions to Worlds. Photos of each version sit beside the changes and lessons from that stage. The reader can see the original intake, understand the pickup and transfer problems, and follow the changes that came next.

This gives the engineering process a clear order. The team explains what it observed and how that observation affected the next build. Small comparisons beside the designs help the reader follow the team's reasoning.

For your own mechanism, save a photo before taking it apart. Put the problem, change, and next test together so readers can follow the same progression.

## STEM connections

Page 6 describes what professionals helped members learn, including design review, manufacturing drawings, electrical documentation, and mechanism ideas. It pairs those relationships with examples of work, so the reader can see why the team sought each connection.

Pages 4–7 also explain the team's wider regional work with schools, industry, and FIRST leadership. They give different kinds of support their own space rather than reducing every relationship to a sponsor logo.

Choose one partnership in your portfolio and show how the advice was used. A drawing, training activity, or revised part can sit beside the explanation of the person's contribution.

## Technical illustrations

The mechanism pages use labelled parts, dimensions, and close-up views. The reader can locate the feature being discussed while reading why it mattered. The autonomous and TeleOp pages also use routes and control diagrams to explain behavior visually.

Use a full-robot view for context and a close-up for the detail you want to explain. Keep the labels near the relevant part and let the text explain the decision behind it.
