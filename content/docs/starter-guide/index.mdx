**FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC)** is a robotics program where students build a robot, work with other teams, and share what they learn. Starting a team also means finding adults to help, planning costs, learning tools, and preparing for events.

Follow the lessons in order, or open the one your team needs today. Technical words are explained where they first appear. The [glossary](./glossary) is there when you need a reminder.

You can begin with paper, a computer, and a few interested people. Some later exercises need a robot kit or access to tools. The CAD exercise runs here in your browser, and the programming exercise uses a simulated robot.
