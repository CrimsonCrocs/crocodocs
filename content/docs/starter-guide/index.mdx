**FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC)** is a robotics program where students build a robot for a game that changes each season. You learn to design, build, and write code together, with adults there to guide you. You can join without having done any of those things before.

Your first season starts with a few interested people and adults who can help. You'll need a place to meet, equipment, a budget, and time to practise. The lessons below take you from those first arrangements to building a robot and attending your first competition.

Begin with **FIRST and FTC**, or use the contents below to find what your team needs now. Read a lesson together in a meeting, then try its activity. You can explore how a part is made and run a robot program here in your browser before working with real hardware.

Technical words are explained as they appear. If you need a reminder, look them up in the [glossary](./glossary).
